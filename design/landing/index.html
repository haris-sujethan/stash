Stash landing page

Direction 5, Vantage, was picked. Variations 6 to 10 each make the cloud mean one Stash feature, with one interaction you can name.

Latest: the flower

  1. 15BubblesBig, dull bubbles arranged like a flower. Agents push, Stash fills, Stash briefs every agent green, they use it and fade back to grey, then push again. Cursor paints a lime trail. Docs page at 15-bubbles/docs.html.
  2. 14Bloom, original layoutEight rounded petals around a seed head. Each petal fires on its own clock, the seed head brightens and fills as pulses land, and when full it sends light back out. Below: laptops pushing to and pulling from the Stash cloud.
  3. 13BloomSixteen pointed petals around a seed head. Every petal streams in at once and the seed head fills in a spiral, then streams back out. The text stays put and swaps as you scroll.
  4. 12Flower, scroll storyA lit 3D flower: petals are agents, the center is Stash, one wave flows in (push) and back out (pull). Scroll and the hero flower itself walks through how Stash works.
  5. 11FlowerPetals are your agents, the center is Stash. Hover a petal and it pushes into the center; click the center and it pulls back out to every petal. Scroll down and the same flower walks through how Stash works.

Vantage variations

  1. 6SearchEach character is one memory, grouped by topic. Ask a question; a sweep passes through and the matching memories turn lime.
  2. 7PushPress push; one session’s memories fall from the command into the topics they belong to, and the memory count grows.
  3. 8PullPick a task and drag the token budget; the memories that make the Briefing light up, and the Briefing fills beside the cloud.
  4. 9MergeTwo clouds, one per session. Drag them together; the same finding overlaps and turns lime, and the conflict stays labelled.
  5. 10GuardrailsA terrain: sessions across, topics deep, failures as peaks. Scrub through eight sessions; a failure seen twice turns lime and proposes a rule.
